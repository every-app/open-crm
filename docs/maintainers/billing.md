# Billing

The first-party CRM on the Every App gateway bills each organization's usage (`src/server/billing`). A fork never bills: its operator's own keys pay the providers.

**One balance with OpenSEO on Every App.** On the hosted Every App gateway, OpenSEO's first-party deployment and the CRM share one Autumn customer, and so one credit balance, per gateway organization. The customer id is the gateway organization id; the acting person's email goes on a new customer. Both apps use the same features and credit math, copied from OpenSEO into `src/shared/billing.ts`: monthly `usage_credits` spent first, then `topup_credits`; 1,000 credits to the dollar; provider cost × 1.28. The CRM's checkout attaches the products OpenSEO sells, the `base-plan` ($10 a month with $10 of credits) and the `credit-top-up`, and a plan that grants `paid_plan` counts as paid in both. Neither app attaches the free plan: each gets or creates the customer, and Autumn gives a new one its default plan. app.openseo.so's customers are separate, in their own Autumn environment, and the CRM never draws on them.

**The secret, and the gateway rule it breaks.** Every App has its own Autumn environment, separate from app.openseo.so's. `everyapp secret set AUTUMN_SECRET_KEY` on the first-party deployment, with that environment's key, the one OpenSEO's Every App deployment uses, never app.openseo.so's. Both apps must use that one environment, or the same organization gets two customers and two balances. It has Stripe connected (checkout and the portal), the free plan as its default, the `base-plan` and `credit-top-up`, and the features `usage_credits`, `topup_credits`, `paid_plan` and `managed_service_access` (the last is OpenSEO's; the CRM doesn't read it). The CRM's other credentials live in the gateway; this one doesn't, on purpose, because the gateway can't bill for its apps yet (no cost reading, no wallet, no Autumn). When the gateway's wallet ships, delete `src/server/billing`, its call sites, `workspace.credits_depleted_at` and `paid_session`, and let the gateway bill.

**No webhook.** The Every App environment sets none. The CRM reads Autumn whenever it needs a balance or a plan, and takes no Autumn events.

**Who bills.** The first-party tier (`src/server/lib/deployment-tier.ts`), outside local dev: `billingEnabledFor` in `src/server/billing/hosted.ts`. `everyapp dev` pins no organization, so a local run derives first party too, and bills only with `BILLING=on` in `.dev.vars`. Billing fails closed: with no Autumn key, or no balance reading, paid work doesn't start. Every charge carries `properties`: `app: "open-crm"`, `provider`, `operation`, `creditFeature` (`crm_agent` for model spend, Sonar searches and compaction summaries; `company_data` for Context.dev), and the session or task it was for.

**Estimates** (`estimates.ts`). What work may cost before it starts. A session's estimate is $0.05 of model calls (a little above the most an OpenSEO SAM turn on GPT Luna cost in 30 days, $0.044; p50 $0.006, p99 $0.024) plus its research budget at Context.dev's list price, $0.025 a unit. A logo lookup is $0.025 and a photo $0.075. In credits: a chat turn 192, a research session 64 to 384 by its budget, a team agent's run 128, a logo 32, a photo 96. Work is billed at what it really cost, so an estimate only paces how much starts on one balance reading.

**Before paid work starts** (`credits.ts`). The agent needs the monthly plus top-up balance to cover a chat turn's estimate. Below it, the organization is out of credits for the agent. A /check reading that falls short is confirmed against Autumn's customer object before anything is refused (OpenSEO's rule), since Autumn has served stale balances.

- A person's chat or builder turn: `SessionMeter.admit` in `CrmAgent.beforeTurn`. Out of credits, the turn answers with a static reply and costs nothing.
- Enrich now and bulk enrich, in the app and the MCP tools (`CompanyService`, `ContactService`), and Run now and Retry (`AgentRunService`): refused with `INSUFFICIENT_CREDITS`. A schedule's or an event's run is skipped and audited (`run.skipped`).
- The minute cron's lanes and pokes (`agent/dispatch.ts`, `team-agents/runtime/dispatch.ts`): each organization's balance is read once a tick, and each task is admitted against what is left. It takes its estimate off; a task that doesn't fit goes back to the queue, its attempt uncounted, and cheaper work behind it may still fit. A run that doesn't fit waits queued; a run whose organization is out of credits fails, as before.
- Autumn's confirmed "out" is kept on the workspace row (`creditReading.ts`) and holds the organization's paid background work for five minutes without asking Autumn again. Reading the Billing page once the balance covers a chat turn lifts the hold at once. The queue and a record's agent tab say the agent waits for credits.

**Sessions at once** (`paidSessions.ts`). An organization runs at most three chat turns, three research sessions and three team agent runs at once. Work admitted on one reading spends before it is billed, so this bounds how far an organization can go past zero. Each session holds a numbered `paid_session` slot from its start until its turn ends (`SessionMeter.flush`), or until its lease runs out: 20 minutes for a chat turn or a run, a research task's 30. A claim is a conditional upsert on the slot number in the key, so the cap is exact on both backends without locks. The dispatcher claims a research session's or a run's slot as it starts it, and work over the cap waits in the queue. A person's chat turn waits up to a minute for a slot, then answers "Other chats in this workspace are still answering. Try again in a minute."

**After it ran.** Spend is billed after the fact, past zero too.

- A session's model steps, compaction summaries and Sonar searches (`meter.ts`): added to the session's running total and billed in $0.05 chunks on one serialized chain, the rest when the turn ends (`onChatResponse`, `onChatError`, kept alive with `waitUntil`). Everything bills with OpenSEO's background overdraft (Autumn's `overflow`), chats included: spend past zero is the organization's debt, and the agent starts nothing until credits cover a turn again. A chat draws on the reading that admitted it; research sessions and team agent runs read the balance for each charge and never throw (`researchSpend.ts`). A step's cost is what OpenRouter charged for the call, so it already holds a builder chat's image and PDF input, OpenRouter's parsing of a PDF for a model that can't read one, and its web searches.
- Sonar and Context.dev report each call from inside the tool that made it (`spendSink.ts`): the agent's tools run inside their session's sink (`tools/registry.ts`), the cron's lookups inside the lane's.
- Context.dev: what it reports in `key_metadata.credits_consumed`, at $0.0025 a credit, else its list price (`contextDevPricing.ts`), as `company_data`, with overdraft.

**Why chats bill the overdraft rather than lock credits.** Autumn can hold credits up front (`check` with a `lock`, settled by `balances.finalize`), and OpenSEO does that for DataForSEO's fixed-price calls. A chat turn's cost isn't known until its steps run. Its real upper bound, 24 steps of up to 160,000 input and 16,000 output tokens plus four research units, is many times what a turn usually costs, so holding it would refuse turns a balance could easily pay for, and a typical estimate would under-reserve anyway. A lock is settled only by `finalize`, which a turn killed by the Durable Object's memory limit never calls: its hold would lapse with nothing deducted, which is the unbilled spend this closes. The meter's chunks bill a killed turn as it goes. A lock also holds one feature, so a turn that spans monthly and top-up credits would need two, and every turn would add Autumn round trips. Billing the overdraft keeps every cent billed with no new Autumn calls, and the turn estimate and the session cap bound how far past zero a balance can go.

**Settings → Billing** (hosted only): the plan with OpenSEO's Upgrade (Autumn checkout) and Manage subscription (Stripe's portal), monthly and top-up credits, Buy credits for $10–$99, and the last 30 days' usage by feature, OpenSEO's included. Only the owner acts (`billing: ["manage"]`). The low and out-of-credits banner is OpenSEO's. Every refusal for credits opens one dialog with OpenSEO's offer (`OutOfCreditsDialog.tsx`), dismissed each time: a refused action through the mutation cache (`INSUFFICIENT_CREDITS`), and a chat turn when its refusal reply arrives in the open chat.

**Usage names.** Both billing pages break usage down by `creditFeature` and show a key they don't know as "Other". OpenSEO's names the CRM's `crm_agent` ("CRM Agent") and `company_data` ("Company Data"); the CRM's names OpenSEO's in its words (`src/shared/billing-credit-features.ts`). A new feature in either app needs its label in both.

## Run it locally

```bash
# .dev.vars
BILLING=on
AUTUMN_DEV_STUB=free   # or paid, low, out
```

`AUTUMN_DEV_STUB` stands in for Autumn (`autumnDevStub.ts`), and only under `everyapp dev`: the organization in one fixed state, nothing charged, checkout coming straight back. `low` is below a chat turn's estimate, so the agent is out of credits there. Without it, `BILLING=on` calls Autumn with `AUTUMN_SECRET_KEY`; use a sandbox key.
